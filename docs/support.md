# Stasher — Support

Stasher is a shared home inventory: write down where you put something,
and everyone in your household can find it.

**Need help, found a bug, or want to request something?**
Email **stasherdotapp@gmail.com** — it reaches a real person.

---

## Common questions

### How do I share my household with someone?

Tap the 👥 button on the home screen to see your invite code, and share
it with them. On their phone, they sign in, choose "Join with a code", and
enter it. You'll both see the same items from then on, updating on each
other's phones within seconds.

### Someone has my invite code and I don't want them to use it

Tap 👥 and choose **Generate a new code**. The old code stops working
right away.

Note that this doesn't remove anyone who has *already* joined — it only
stops the old code being used again. If you need to remove someone who is
already in your household, email us.

### Can I use Stasher on my own?

Yes. Create a household and never share the code. Everything works the
same; you're just the only member.

### Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Adding and editing items works offline — useful, since the back of a
closet or a basement often has no signal. Changes are saved on your phone
straight away and sync when you're back online.

### I left my household by accident

Rejoin with the same invite code and everything comes back. Leaving
doesn't delete anything, even if you were the last person in the
household.

If you don't have the code any more, email us before doing anything else
and we'll help you get back in.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: 👥 button on the home screen → **Delete account**. It happens
immediately.

If you're the only person left in your household, the household and
everything in it is deleted too. If other people are still in it, their
items stay — those belong to the household rather than to you.

This can't be undone.

### Who can see what I write in a note?

Everyone in your household. Items and notes are shared with every member,
so treat a note as something the whole house can read.

Nobody outside your household can see any of it.

### I found a bug

In the app: **?** button on the home screen → **Submit feedback / report
a bug**. That opens an email with your version details already filled in,
which saves a round trip asking which build you're on.

Or just email **stasherdotapp@gmail.com** directly. What helps most:
what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
Screenshots are welcome.

### How do I see the tour again?

**?** button on the home screen → **Tutorial**. It's the same walkthrough
shown when a household is first set up, and you can leave it at any point
with **Skip tour**.

### Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics in Stasher, and
nothing is sold or shared with anyone. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy) for the full detail.
